Latch runs if/then automation on your phone without accounts, ads, or cloud sync.

• Triggers: battery, Wi‑Fi/SSID, Bluetooth, headphones, time, boot, apps
• Actions: notify, ringer, brightness, open app/URL, shell (optional root/Shizuku)
• JSON import/export of recipes
• Material You theme
• No INTERNET permission

FOSS (MIT). Optional elevation is always explicit.
