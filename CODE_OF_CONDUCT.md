# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insults, or publishing others’ private information.

## Enforcement

Report issues to **acartoon766@gmail.com**. Maintainers may remove contributions or ban participants who violate this code.
